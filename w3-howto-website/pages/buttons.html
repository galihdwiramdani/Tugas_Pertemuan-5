How To - Alert Buttons

❮ PreviousNext ❯

Tombol berwarna untuk status sukses, info, peringatan, dan bahaya.

Satu kelas dasar .btn dipakai bersama, lalu setiap status hanya mengganti warna latar.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<style>
.btn{border:0;color:#fff;padding:14px 28px;font-size:16px;cursor:pointer;border-radius:3px}
.success{background:#04AA6D}
.info{background:#2196F3}
.warning{background:#ff9800}
.danger{background:#f44336}
.default{background:#e7e7e7;color:#000}
.btn:hover{opacity:.8}
</style>
<button class="btn success">Success</button>
<button class="btn info">Info</button>
<button class="btn warning">Warning</button>
<button class="btn danger">Danger</button>
<button class="btn default">Default</button>

How To - Hover Dropdowns

❮ PreviousNext ❯

Menu dropdown yang muncul saat kursor diarahkan ke tombol.

Isi dropdown disembunyikan dengan display:none, lalu ditampilkan lewat selector :hover. Tidak perlu JavaScript.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<style>
body{font-family:Arial}
.dd{position:relative;display:inline-block}
.dd button{background:#04AA6D;color:#fff;padding:14px 22px;border:0;font-size:16px}
.dd-menu{display:none;position:absolute;background:#f1f1f1;min-width:160px}
.dd-menu a{display:block;padding:12px 16px;color:#000;text-decoration:none}
.dd-menu a:hover{background:#ddd}
.dd:hover .dd-menu{display:block}
</style>
<div class="dd">
  <button>Hover</button>
  <div class="dd-menu">
    <a href="#">Link 1</a>
    <a href="#">Link 2</a>
    <a href="#">Link 3</a>
  </div>
</div>
